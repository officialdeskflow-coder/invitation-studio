# What to collect from a couple

**Names and date**
- Names exactly as they want them spelled (and the Gurmukhi/Hindi wording for the greeting line, if any)
- Wedding date, ceremony start time, time zone
- Welcome message (or accept the default) and any family blessing line

**Events** (max 8): time, name, place for each

**Venue**: venue name, city, exact Google Maps link (or an address I can search)

**Photos**: 3 portrait photos, high resolution, faces in the upper half, plus written permission to use them online

**RSVP**: who receives replies (the Google Sheet owner), maximum guests per reply, reply-by date

**Optional**: a song (and confirmation they have the rights to use it), language preferences, sections to leave out

**Delivery**: deadline, who approves the preview, who sends the link to guests, whether they want the link live until the wedding date or a month after

Delivery checklist: see the end of `docs/ADD-A-CLIENT.md`.
